## Round 1 draft

Once upon a time, in a peaceful village nestled between rolling hills and a sparkling river, there lived a scary dragon. The villagers had heard tales of the dragon's fiery breath and sharp claws, and they trembled in fear whenever its shadow passed overhead.

One bright morning, a touch of magic fluttered through the village, creating a small surprise near the edge of the forest. Curiosity sparked in the villagers' hearts as they gathered to see what the magic had brought. To their surprise, the scary dragon stood before them, not with flames and roars, but with a gentle smile and kind eyes.

The villagers, at first hesitant, approached the dragon with courage born from curiosity. They discovered that the dragon loved to paint colorful murals on the walls of caves and tell stories of faraway lands. The once scary dragon now became a beloved friend, bringing joy and wonder to the village with its talents.

One day, a young child in the village fell ill, and the villagers were worried. The dragon, sensing their concern, used its magical breath to create healing herbs that cured the child's sickness. Gratitude filled the villagers' hearts as they realized that the dragon's magic was not meant to harm, but to help and heal.

As the sun dipped below the horizon, casting a warm glow over the village, the dragon and the villagers gathered around a crackling fire. They shared stories, laughter, and delicious treats under the starry sky. The once fearful villagers now felt a deep sense of peace and friendship with the dragon, knowing that true courage was found in understanding and kindness.

With hearts full of warmth and bellies full of treats, the villagers bid the dragon goodnight. Slowly, the village grew quiet, lulled by the gentle rustle of leaves and the soft murmur of the river. The stars twinkled overhead, casting a soft glow over the sleeping village, where the dragon and the villagers rested peacefully, dreaming of painted murals and magical tales.
